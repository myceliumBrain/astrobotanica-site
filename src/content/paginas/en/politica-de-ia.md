Astrobotânica discloses, on every news story, podcast episode and study entry, whether any artificial intelligence (A.I.) tool was used to produce the content, and how.

## 1. What is disclosed

At the end of each piece there is a "Use of artificial intelligence" box. When no tool was used, the box says so. When one was, it states:

- **Tool:** its name and, when possible, the version and date of use.
- **Writing:** whether the content was made entirely by the author, by the author with A.I. assistance, or entirely by A.I.
- **How A.I. was used:** for example reference search, spelling and grammar check, translation, audio transcription or image generation.
- **Human review:** whether the content was reviewed and fact-checked by a person, and by whom.

A.I.-generated images are labeled one by one, right below the image.

## 2. Content without a disclosure

Content published before this policy has no box. This does not mean A.I. was or was not used: the information was simply not recorded at the time.

## 3. Responsibility

A.I. tools do not replace reading the original sources. All published content is the responsibility of the credited authors, who answer for its accuracy. Errors are corrected according to the [Editorial Policy](/en/politica-editorial).
