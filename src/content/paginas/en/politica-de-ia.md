<p style="text-align: justify">The site www.astrobotanica.com.br discloses, on every news story, podcast episode and study entry, whether any artificial intelligence (A.I.) tool was used to produce the content, and how.</p>

<h2 style="text-align: justify">1. What is disclosed</h2>

<p style="text-align: justify">At the end of each piece there is a "Use of artificial intelligence" box. When no tool was used, the box says so. When one was, it states:</p>

<ul>
<li style="text-align: justify"><strong>Tool:</strong> its name and, when possible, the version and date of use.</li>
<li style="text-align: justify"><strong>Writing:</strong> whether the content was made by the author with A.I. assistance, or entirely by A.I.</li>
<li style="text-align: justify"><strong>How A.I. was used:</strong> for example reference search, spelling and grammar check, translation, drafting of the text, audio transcription or image generation.</li>
<li style="text-align: justify"><strong>Human review:</strong> By whom the content was reviewed.</li>
</ul>

<p style="text-align: justify">A.I. generated images are labeled one by one, right below the image.</p>

<h2 style="text-align: justify">2. Responsibility</h2>

<p style="text-align: justify">A.I. tools do not replace reading the original sources. All published content is the responsibility of the credited authors, who answer for its accuracy. Errors are corrected according to the <a href="/en/politica-editorial">Editorial Policy</a>.</p>
