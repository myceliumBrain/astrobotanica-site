The site www.astrobotanica.com.br publishes only content based on verifiable scientific sources, following a defined editorial process that includes public corrections and the disclosure of any conflicts of interest.

## 1. Scope

We publish content regarding plants, fungi, and microorganisms in space and extreme environments, including space agriculture, regolith simulants, microgravity, life-support systems, plant-microorganism interactions, and related topics, such as public policy and the space economy as they pertain to cultivation. We do not publish promotional content or opinion pieces presented as news.

## 2. Content Types

- **Scientific News: **Coverage of a published study, presented in accessible language.
- **Podcast:** A format focused on discussion, reflection, and entertainment.

## 2.1 Planned Content

- **Interview:** A conversation with researchers, reviewed by the interviewee prior to publication.
- **Guest Article:** A text written by a researcher about their field or work, clearly identified as such.
- **Supporting Material: **Glossaries, introductory texts, and resources for educators.
- **Expert Opinion:** Clearly labeled as an opinion piece and signed by the author.

## 3. Sources and Rigor

Every text cites the original source, including a link and a DOI, where available.

We specify the nature of the study, whether it is peer-reviewed or a preprint, and whether it was conducted in a laboratory, using a simulant, or during a suborbital or orbital flight.

We do not extrapolate results beyond what the study demonstrates, and relevant limitations are noted.

Whenever possible, we consult the study’s authors or an independent expert.

Headlines must accurately reflect the content, avoiding sensationalism.

## 4. Editorial Process

Topic proposals must be approved by the editorial board (comprising members and invited contributors to the www.astrobotanica.com.br project).

Drafting, with all sources documented.

Editorial review (focusing on clarity, language, and standards).

Technical review by a member of the Scientific Advisory Board or an invited expert for texts covering topics outside the author’s area of ​​expertise or those of greater complexity. Publication including date, authorship, and, where applicable, the name of the technical reviewer.

## 5. Use of artificial intelligence

AI tools may assist with spell checking and translation. They do not replace the reading of original sources, and all published content is verified by and remains the responsibility of human authors.

## 6. Corrections

Errors are corrected as soon as they are identified. Corrections that alter relevant information are noted at the end of the text, including the date and a description of the change. Content is never silently deleted. To report an error, please write to **contato@astrobotanica.com.br**

## 7. Independence and Conflicts of Interest

Astrobotânica does not accept payment to publish or refrain from publishing any content.

If a text discusses research in which the author, editor, or a member of the Advisory Board is involved, this is disclosed within the text itself.

## 8. Copyright and Reproduction

Original content is published under a CC BY 4.0 license: it may be reproduced and adapted, including for use in the classroom, provided that the source is cited. Images from third parties remain subject to their own respective licenses, which are indicated in their captions and references.

## 9. Privacy

This website does not use tracking cookies, and no registration is required to read, listen to, or share the content. We collect only aggregated access statistics without the use of cookies to determine which texts are the most read. Further details, including rights guaranteed under the [LGPD (General Personal Data Protection Law)](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709compilado.htm), can be found in our [Privacy Policy](/Privacidade).
