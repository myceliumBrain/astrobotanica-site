Researchers, graduate students, journalists, and undergraduate students are invited to propose articles based on their own work or on topics within the scope of Astrobotanica (see our [Editorial Policy)](/en/politica-editorial).

**How to submit a proposal:** Please send an email to **contato@astrobotanica.com.br** containing a paragraph that outlines the topic, the target audience, and key references. We will respond within 10 days.

**Article format**

- **Length:** No character limit.
- **Language:** Accessible to non-specialists; technical terms must be explained upon their first use.
- **References:** Listed at the end (including DOIs where available); in-text citations should be linked.
- **Images:** Must be your own or licensed for reproduction; include a caption and proper credit.
- **Author identification:** Name, academic background, institution, ORCID, Lattes CV, and a brief bio. (If any of these items are unavailable, please contact us at **contato@astrobotanica.com.br**)

**Evaluation:** Articles undergo an editorial review and, when necessary, a technical review (see our [Editorial Policy](/en/politica-editorial)). The author must approve the final version prior to publication.

**Rights:** The author retains the rights to their text and authorizes its publication under a CC BY 4.0 license. There are no fees for publication, nor is there any payment provided to the author.

**Conflicts of interest:** Authors must disclose any involvement, funding, or affiliation related to the studies cited.
