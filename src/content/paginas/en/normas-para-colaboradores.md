<p style="text-align: justify">Researchers, graduate students, journalists, and undergraduate students are invited to propose articles based on their own work or on topics within the scope of Astrobotanica (see our <a href="/en/politica-editorial">Editorial Policy)</a>.</p>

<p style="text-align: justify"><strong>How to submit a proposal:</strong> Please send an email to <strong>contato@astrobotanica.com.br</strong> containing a paragraph that outlines the topic, the target audience, and key references. We will respond within 10 days.</p>

<p style="text-align: justify"><strong>Article format</strong></p>

<ul>
<li style="text-align: justify"><strong>Length:</strong> No character limit.</li>
<li style="text-align: justify"><strong>Language:</strong> Accessible to non-specialists; technical terms must be explained upon their first use.</li>
<li style="text-align: justify"><strong>References:</strong> Listed at the end (including DOIs where available); in-text citations should be linked.</li>
<li style="text-align: justify"><strong>Images:</strong> Must be your own or licensed for reproduction; include a caption and proper credit.</li>
<li style="text-align: justify"><strong>Author identification:</strong> Name, academic background, institution, ORCID, Lattes CV, and a brief bio. (If any of these items are unavailable, please contact us at <strong>contato@astrobotanica.com.br</strong>)</li>
</ul>

<p style="text-align: justify"><strong>Evaluation:</strong> Articles undergo an editorial review and, when necessary, a technical review (see our <a href="/en/politica-editorial">Editorial Policy</a>). The author must approve the final version prior to publication.</p>

<p style="text-align: justify"><strong>Rights:</strong> The author retains the rights to their text and authorizes its publication under a CC BY 4.0 license. There are no fees for publication, nor is there any payment provided to the author.</p>

<p style="text-align: justify"><strong>Conflicts of interest:</strong> Authors must disclose any involvement, funding, or affiliation related to the studies cited.</p>
