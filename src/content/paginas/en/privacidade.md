<p style="text-align: justify">This policy explains how Astrobotânica handles the personal data of visitors to <strong>astrobotanica.com.br</strong>, in accordance with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (<a href="https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709compilado.htm">LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018</a>).</p>

<h2 style="text-align: justify">In short</h2>

<ul>
<li style="text-align: justify">The site <strong>does not use tracking cookies</strong>, nor advertising or social media cookies.</li>
<li style="text-align: justify">You don't need an account or to provide any personal data to read, listen to or share the content.</li>
<li style="text-align: justify">We do not sell or hand over visitor data to anyone.</li>
<li style="text-align: justify">We only measure how many times each page is visited, in aggregate and without cookies.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="text-align: justify">Who is responsible</h2>

<p style="text-align: justify">The data controller, under the LGPD, is the Biologist and Science Communicator Pedro Henrique Tenorio Alvarenga (See in <a href="/en/sobre">About</a>). For any matter related to privacy and personal data, write to <strong>contato@astrobotanica.com.br</strong></p>

<h2 style="text-align: justify">Visit statistics</h2>

<p style="text-align: justify">To know which texts are read the most (for example, the "Most read" list), we use <a href="https://www.goatcounter.com/">GoatCounter</a>, an <em>open source statistics service</em> that <strong>does not use cookies</strong> and does not identify individual visitors. For each visit, it records:</p>

<ul>
<li style="text-align: justify">the page visited and the site the visitor came from (if any);</li>
<li style="text-align: justify">the type of browser and operating system, and the screen size;</li>
<li style="text-align: justify">the country of origin, estimated from the IP address. The IP address itself is not stored.</li>
</ul>

<p style="text-align: justify">We use this data only as totals (number of visits per page), based on our legitimate interest in understanding the reach of the content (art. 7, IX, LGPD).</p>

<h2 style="text-align: justify">Hosting</h2>

<p style="text-align: justify">The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., in the United States. Like any server, it records technical data about requests, such as the IP address, to keep the service secure and running, according to the <a href="https://docs.github.com/pt/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement">GitHub Privacy Statement</a>. This involves an international data transfer, under art. 33 of the LGPD.</p>

<h2 style="text-align: justify">Fonts</h2>

<p style="text-align: justify">The site's fonts (Barlow, Barlow Condensed and IBM Plex Mono) are served by astrobotanica.com.br itself. Your browser does not connect to third-party font services such as Google Fonts.</p>

<h2 style="text-align: justify">External links and sharing</h2>

<p style="text-align: justify">The share buttons are plain links: no information is sent to social networks until you click one of them. When you follow a link to another site (social networks, scientific articles, references), that site's privacy policy applies.</p>

<h2 style="text-align: justify">Contact by e-mail</h2>

<p style="text-align: justify">If you write to us, we will use your e-mail address and the content of your message only to reply. We never add anyone to mailing lists without an express request.</p>

<h2 style="text-align: justify">Your rights</h2>

<p style="text-align: justify">The LGPD (art. 18) guarantees you, among others, the right to:</p>

<ul>
<li style="text-align: justify">confirm whether we process your data and access it;</li>
<li style="text-align: justify">correct incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;</li>
<li style="text-align: justify">request the anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data;</li>
<li style="text-align: justify">know with whom your data has been shared;</li>
<li style="text-align: justify">object to processing based on legitimate interest.</li>
</ul>

<p style="text-align: justify">To exercise these rights, write to <strong>contato@astrobotanica.com.br</strong>; You may also file a complaint with Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (<a href="https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/canais_atendimento">ANPD</a>).</p>
