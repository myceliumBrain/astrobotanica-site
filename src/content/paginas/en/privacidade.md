This policy explains how Astrobotânica handles the personal data of visitors to **astrobotanica.com.br**, in accordance with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018).

## In short

- The site **does not use tracking cookies**, nor advertising or social media cookies.
- You don't need an account or to provide any personal data to read, listen to or share the content.
- We do not sell or hand over visitor data to anyone.
- We only measure how many times each page is visited, in aggregate and without cookies.

## Who is responsible

The data controller, under the LGPD, is [name of the person responsible for the site]. For any matter related to privacy and personal data, write to [privacy contact e-mail].

## Visit statistics

To know which texts are read the most (for example, the "Most read" list), we use [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/), a statistics service that **does not use cookies** and does not identify individual visitors. For each visit, it records:

- the page visited and the site the visitor came from (if any);
- the type of browser and operating system, and the screen size;
- the country of origin, estimated from the IP address. The IP address itself is not stored.

We use this data only as totals (number of visits per page), based on our legitimate interest in understanding the reach of the content (art. 7, IX, LGPD).

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., in the United States. Like any server, it records technical data about requests, such as the IP address, to keep the service secure and running, according to the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). This involves an international data transfer, under art. 33 of the LGPD.

## Fonts

The site's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. To do so, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser information, according to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en). Google Fonts does not set cookies.

## External links and sharing

The share buttons are plain links: no information is sent to social networks until you click one of them. When you follow a link to another site (social networks, scientific articles, references), that site's privacy policy applies.

## Contact by e-mail

If you write to us, we will use your e-mail address and the content of your message only to reply. We never add anyone to mailing lists without an express request.

## Your rights

The LGPD (art. 18) guarantees you, among others, the right to:

- confirm whether we process your data and access it;
- correct incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
- request the anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data;
- know with whom your data has been shared;
- object to processing based on legitimate interest.

To exercise these rights, write to [privacy contact e-mail]. You may also file a complaint with Brazil's National Data Protection Authority ([ANPD](https://www.gov.br/anpd/)).
