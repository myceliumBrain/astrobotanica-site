**Astrobotanica is a Brazilian science communication platform dedicated to astrobotany**: The study of plants, space agriculture, astronomy, technology, growth substrates, microorganisms, and other related topics, with a particular focus on space and extreme environments.

The project was launched by biologist Pedro Alvarenga as an individual initiative, following his entry into the Laboratory of Plant and Fungal Molecular Biology (LBMPF) at the Federal University of the State of Rio de Janeiro (UNIRIO), which is directed by Dr. Camila Maistro Patreze.

Content is published in both Portuguese and English, prioritizing an accessible yet in-depth approach. The website currently features news coverage of recent and significant scientific papers in the field, alongside a **podcast titled Jardim em Expansão **("Expanding Garden"), which explores astrobotany through discussion, reflection, and entertainment.

Mission: To raise awareness of astrobotany in Brazil, bridging the gap between national and international research and the general public, schools, and the scientific community.
